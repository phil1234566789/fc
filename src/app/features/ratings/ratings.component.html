Attribute Ratings

What each number actually means on the pitch.

OVR

Overall Rating

The average of all 7 attributes, rounded to a whole number:

(PAC + SHO + PAS + DRI + DEF + PHY + TEC) ÷ 7

Every attribute weighs equally. In 5v5 there are no fixed positions — everyone attacks and defends, so a high SHO doesn't offset a low DEF. A player who never tracks back is always one overload in defence.

TOT

Total Score

The raw sum of all 7 attributes. Maximum possible: 700 (7 × 100).

PAC + SHO + PAS + DRI + DEF + PHY + TEC

Useful when two players share the same OVR — the total breaks the tie. A player with OVR 75 / TOT 530 is objectively stronger than OVR 75 / TOT 519.
